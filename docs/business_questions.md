# Business Questions

## Performance

1. What are total advertising spend and reported revenue?
2. What is blended ROAS for the selected period?
3. How does ROAS change by day and week?
4. Are there dates where reported revenue is below spend?

## Allocation

5. Which accounts and campaigns consume the largest share of spend?
6. Does spend concentration create material exposure to one account or campaign?
7. Are high-ROAS segments supported by enough spend and volume to justify further testing?

## Budget control

8. How does actual spend compare with the budget reference?
9. Are pacing differences isolated or persistent?
10. Is the budget defined at the same grain as the spend data?

## Data reliability

11. Is the reporting grain understood and consistent?
12. Are there missing dates, duplicate keys, ambiguous account labels, or zero-spend records with revenue?
13. Could attribution timing or incomplete tracking distort ROAS?

The questions should be answered in this order: validate the data, establish the overall baseline, locate meaningful differences, then recommend investigation or action.
